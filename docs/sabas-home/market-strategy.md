## Task 1 — Market analysis & audience

Alanya is Türkiye's 3rd-most-populous district (**361,873**, 2024, +5.5% YoY) with an unusually large foreign-resident base: **36,465 registered foreign residents (10.08% of the district, 2024)** — Russians **32.1%** (11,720), Ukrainians 13.9%, **Germans 10.7%** (3,902), British 7.7%, Kazakhs 6.6%. Antalya province holds **132,302 foreign permit-holders**, 2nd in Türkiye. Tourism is enormous: **17.28M Antalya arrivals (2024)** (Russians 3.9M, Germans 3.52M), >25% vacationing in Alanya, ~668 hotels / ~150,000 beds. Antalya was the **2nd province for foreign house sales in 2024 (8,223 of 23,781 nationally)**; Alanya's cumulative foreign-purchased housing stock 2015–2023 ≈ **53,830 units**. A new foreign owner spends ~**TL 200,000** furnishing a home yet is present only ~**2.8 months/yr** — driving demand for delivered, turnkey, remotely-coordinated furnishing. Expat guides explicitly flag the **language barrier** in furniture/appliance shopping as a purchase hurdle.

**Demand drivers** — Verified: second-home furnishing spend; large/growing foreign-resident base; deep foreign-owned housing stock; tourism/hospitality FF&E; year-round (velvet-season) demand; documented language barrier; growing Turkish furniture market (~US$8.9bn by 2029) with rising e-commerce. Hypothesis (not verified): move-in seasonality concentrates furnishing months; local/foreign spending bifurcation.

**Why RU/EN/TR/DE:** TR = local majority + B2B; RU = largest resident group + #1 tourist market + #1 foreign homebuyer; DE = large resident/retiree community + #2 tourist market; EN = lingua franca for British/Ukrainian/Kazakh/Scandinavian buyers.

## Task 3 — Gaps & international benchmarks

**Verified gaps Sabaş can own:**
1. **Multilingual (RU/EN/TR/DE) web + service** — the single clearest differentiator in a Russian/German/expat-heavy market where nearly all rivals are Turkish-only. *(This new homepage closes it.)*
2. **Room/use-case browsing** — no local rival guides shoppers by the space they're furnishing.
3. **Quantified trust presented as numbers**, not slogans — Sabaş owns strong facts (20+ yrs, 5 stores, 41 brands, 5 districts, e-commerce on 4 channels) it currently under-uses.
4. **One-stop turnkey furnishing narrative** for second-home owners/investors (white goods + beds + carpets + décor, delivered) — a story no competitor tells clearly.
5. **AI multilingual concierge** — nobody in the local market offers grounded, 24/7 multilingual guidance.

**International benchmarks:**
- **JYSK** (jysk.co.uk) — room-first nav + a compact strip of quantified promises: "3600 stores in 49 countries", "25-year guarantee", unlimited returns, "everyday low price", "Est. Denmark 1979". *Emulate:* room-first entry + numeric trust strip built from owned facts.
- **IKEA** (ikea.com/gb/en/rooms) — "Shop by room" image-tile grid as the primary browsing path + headline numeric guarantees (365-day returns, 3–25-yr guarantee). *Emulate:* a room/use-case tile grid for a mixed catalogue exactly like Sabaş's.

- **A. Digital presence:** Optimise the 5 Google Business Profiles (photos, hours, attributes, RU/EN/DE posts). For walk-in stores, "mobilya / beyaz eşya / halı Alanya" Maps visibility matters as much as the site. *Recommendation.*

Run an everyday QR-at-handover / consent-based WhatsApp follow-up to grow **real** reviews (never bought/incentivised-undisclosed). Build the new (Alaiye) listing's first reviews. *Recommendation.*
- **C. Brand & CI:** Keep the existing CI (logo, ink #0f172a + amber #f38a22 + azure #2999d3, Playfair Display + Montserrat) and apply it consistently — a cleaner modern reinterpretation is used on this homepage. *Recommendation to standardise across facade/signage, social grid, PDFs.*
- **D. Content & imagery:** Photo producibility = *quickly producible* (5 physical stores). Use the client's real store/interior photos (used here) and replace any interim visuals with real ones; never present representative/AI media as real client work.
- **E. Differentiation:** Lead with **multilingual + one-stop-furnishing + curated European brands**, the verified gaps above.
- **F. Portfolio bundling & price positioning:** Propose room/turnkey packages ("Furnish a rental", "New-home starter") bundling white goods+beds+carpets+décor, and a B2B/hospitality supply track. Mark all packages/prices as **Recommendation** until approved; do **not** publish prices unverified.
- **G. Local SEO & language:** Human-facing RU/EN/TR/DE switching now (this build); add crawlable per-language URLs + `hreflang` as a follow-up for true multilingual SEO. Prioritise TR + RU, then DE + EN.
